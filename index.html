Profile

ABOUT

WooferLOOPer is an electronic music creator and sound designer dedicated to exploring the sonic possibilities of hardware synthesizers and grooveboxes. Primarily active on YouTube and X, he specializes in “Dawless” machine live performances—crafting intricate soundscapes and driving beats entirely without the use of a computer.

His setup features a diverse array of gear, including the YAMAHA SEQTRAK, KORG electribe2, Novation Circuit, and SONICWARE Ambient0. By manipulating knobs and faders in real-time, he creates sounds that span a wide range of genres, from energetic techno and electro to immersive, cinematic ambient music.

Check out his latest performances and gear explorations on YouTube, and get a behind-the-scenes look at his creative process on X. Join WooferLOOPer and dive into a world of profound electronic sound infused with an analog sensibility.
